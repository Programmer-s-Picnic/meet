How to join

  1. Both people press Start Camera.
  2. The teacher creates and sends the meeting offer.
  3. The student pastes the offer and creates an answer.
  4. The student sends the answer back.
  5. The teacher pastes it and presses Connect Using Answer.

No application server stores or relays the meeting. This prototype uses a public STUN service to help the browsers establish a direct WebRTC connection. Some restrictive networks may still require a TURN server.
